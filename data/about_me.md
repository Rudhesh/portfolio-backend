# About Me – Rudesh Dhote

## Who I Am

I'm Rudesh Dhote, a software developer originally from India and currently living in Germany.

I'm someone who enjoys learning, exploring new ideas, experimenting with technology, and solving real-world problems. My journey has been anything but conventional. I've explored engineering, theatre, graphic design, e-commerce, digital marketing, and software development. Each experience has taught me something different and helped shape the person I am today.

I believe that life is not about following just one path. It's about being curious, taking opportunities, learning from experiences, and having the courage to start something new.

## My Journey from India to Germany

I was born in Madhya Pradesh, India. I completed my early education there and later moved to Indore for my higher secondary education and engineering entrance preparation.

I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at GH Raisoni College in Pune.

Although I had an engineering background, I was also strongly interested in creativity, acting, and storytelling. This interest took me to Mumbai, where I explored theatre and worked on projects in the Bollywood and television industry, including creative and casting-related roles.

I also discovered an interest in business and entrepreneurship. I worked in graphic design, e-commerce, product catalogues, photography, and online advertising. I gained practical experience helping online sellers promote their products on platforms such as Amazon and Flipkart.

These experiences gave me a broader understanding of creativity, business, marketing, and technology.

Eventually, I decided to move to Germany because I wanted a better future and saw an opportunity to build a new life here.

I came to Germany ready to learn, adapt, and take on new challenges. I appreciate the culture, the working environment, and the opportunities this country offers. Over time, Germany has become an important part of my life.

## My Professional Journey

My professional journey combines creative and technical experience.

Before becoming a software developer, I worked in graphic design, digital content, e-commerce, and advertising. I spent several years working with creative teams, developing product catalogues, preparing photography, editing content, and helping businesses improve their online presence.

I later transitioned into software development.

I studied Full Stack Web Development at the Digital Career Institute in Berlin, where I developed skills in JavaScript, React, Node.js, databases, and modern web technologies.

I completed an internship in IT recruitment and then worked as a software developer at Breeze Technologies in Hamburg. Later, I joined Breitfuss Messtechnik in Harpstedt, Lower Saxony, where I worked on an analytics dashboard using Next.js and Tailwind CSS.

I've also continued improving my German language skills and am currently working towards B2.

My career has taught me that changing direction is not necessarily starting from zero. Every previous experience can become useful in a new field.
